# Future research: individual employment-outcome models

Documented only. **Not implemented.**

Do not construct an employment-outcome model from occupation exposure, resume text, occupation decline, employment counts, or annual openings. Do not create synthetic individual outcomes. Do not treat `occupation decline = individual job loss`. Do not use an E0/E1/E2 category as a proxy unemployment target.

A genuine employment-outcome model would require:

- a clearly defined observed outcome
- longitudinal labelled data
- person-period records
- time-based train/test evaluation
- calibration
- external validation
- subgroup evaluation
- privacy review
- fairness review
- distribution-shift monitoring
- uncertainty reporting

The future target must be an observed employment outcome, not an exposure score copied from another benchmark.
