# Career-action layer (non-predictive)

General career-development guidance. Not derived from an individual's probability of job loss. No listed action guarantees employment.

- Strengthen complementary skills.
- Document measurable work outcomes.
- Build evidence of domain knowledge.
- Develop stakeholder communication skills.
- Develop verification and quality-control practices.
- Build responsible AI-use capabilities.
- Strengthen judgment and exception-handling capabilities.

This layer may sit beside contextual task exposure and task-overlap descriptions. It must not be described as a remedy for predicted unemployment.
