How It Works

AI-powered career intelligence, built for explainability and privacy.

Prayash combines local machine learning, O*NET occupational data, and optional AI narrative generation to surface automation risk and reskilling pathways—all without storing your resume data permanently.

Our approach

We process resumes locally, vectorize using industry-standard TF-IDF, predict risk with scikit-learn, and optionally generate narratives using Llama 3.

Flask Backend scikit-learn Ollama LLM O*NET Data

1. Ingest

Load automation risk profiles, resume corpus samples, and O*NET occupational data. Build a unified vocabulary across all sources.

2. Vectorize

Apply TF-IDF vectorization so your resume text can be compared fairly against job descriptions and occupational clusters.

3. Predict

Compute automation risk and find the top matching roles instantly using local ML. Optionally send results to Llama 3 for deeper insights.

4. Recommend

Generate a personalized learning roadmap by matching your skills against Coursera courses and career clusters.
