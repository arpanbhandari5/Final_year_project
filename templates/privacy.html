Your Privacy

Local-first design means your data stays yours.

Prayash processes resumes in memory, never stores them permanently, and keeps all model artifacts local. Your career intelligence is computed privately.

Core principle

We believe career analysis should be fast, transparent, and private. That's why Prayash uses in-memory processing and local machine learning models.

In-memory processing No permanent storage Local ML models Privacy by design

How your data is protected

Resume handling

Your resume is parsed in memory during analysis. After we return results to your browser, the file is immediately discarded. No backup, no logging, no storage.

Analysis results

Risk scores, skill matches, and career insights are computed locally and sent to your browser as JSON. These results remain under your control.

Model artifacts

Machine learning models are saved locally in the ml_models/ directory. They're used for inference only—they don't track or store user data.

Advanced AI mode

If you enable advanced narrative generation, we send only your already-computed ML results to the local Ollama instance. No resume text leaves your system.

What we do NOT do

  • Create user profiles
  • Track session history
  • Sell resume data
  • Use analytics trackers
  • Require persistent login
  • Build advertising profiles
